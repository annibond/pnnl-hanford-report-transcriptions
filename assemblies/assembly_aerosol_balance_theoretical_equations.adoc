== Aerosol Balance Equations: Theoretical Studies
[.big]#{RLD}#

_Similarity solutions have been obtained for homogeneous and nonhomogeneous aerosol systems. The processes governing these time-evolving systems are coagulation, the condensation of vapors and gases on the particles, volumetric sources and sinks, gravitational setting, vertical velocity fields, and vertical diffusion._
