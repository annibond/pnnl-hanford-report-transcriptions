== A General Scavenging Formalism for the Precipitation Scavenging for Aerosol Particles
[.big]#{WGNS}#

_From a continuity equation, general expressions are derived for the particle- and space-averaged precipitation scavenging rates for an aerosol pollutant. This formalism provided a framework for developing approximations for the removal rates._
