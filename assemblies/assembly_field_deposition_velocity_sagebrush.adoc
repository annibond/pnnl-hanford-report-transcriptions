== Field Deposition Velocity Measurements to a Sagebrush Canopy
[.big]#{GAS} and {WHH}#

_Experimental upwind and downwind air sampling arrays were set up and initial mass budget experiment conducted to measure particle deposition velocities across a 136 m fetch of sagebrush. Particle diameters in the range of 0.7 μm (where deposition velocities should be nearly independent of particle diameter) were used. Plume depletion within the canopy was 65%._
