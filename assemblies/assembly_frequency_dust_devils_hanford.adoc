== Frequency of Dust Devils in the Hanford Reservation*
[.big]#{MMO}, {JMT} and {LCS}#

_Field observations in the Hanford Reservation have shown that about 20 dust devils per hour in a 1-mile area may form under favorable wind, stability and surface conditions. Dust devil activity is more favorable during southwest-through-north wind directions during very unstable periods._
