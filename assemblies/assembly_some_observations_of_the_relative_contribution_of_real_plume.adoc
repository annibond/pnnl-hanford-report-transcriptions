== Some Observations of the Relative Contribution of Real Plume Diffusion and Meander to Apparent Plume Diffusion
[.big]#{PWN}#

_The contribution of real plume diffusion to the apparent plume considered in hazards appraisals varies from less than 10% to more than 80%. No dependence on conventional stability measurements is obvious in the 8 field experiments examined._
