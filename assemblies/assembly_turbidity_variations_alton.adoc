== Turbidity Variations over the Alton, {IL}, Airport During August 1975
[.big]#{NSL}#

_Multiwavelength turidities were derived from sunphotometer at {cair} from July 30, 1975 to August 11, 1975. Aerosol loading varied by an order of magnitude during this time with the highest turbidities associated during fog/haze conditions. The turbidity was found to have a wavelength dependence with Ångström exponent stem:[\alpha] between stem:[1.4] and stem:[2.4]_
