== Field Measurement of the Effect of Filter Orientation on Sampling of a Particulate Tracer
[.big#]{PWN}#

_A particulate tracer was sampled by filters facing upward and filters facing toward and away from the mean wind direction. The toward-source samplers proved most efficient while the upward-facing filters displayed lowest efficiencies. Irrespective of efficiency, aerosol sampling under conditions departing significantly from iskinetic may be invalid._
