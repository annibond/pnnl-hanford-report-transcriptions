== Preparation of Hourly Maps of Gridded Precipitation Data for Use in Precipitation Scavenging Calculations

[.big]#{LLW} and {CEH}#

_A method for producing hourly fields of gridded precipitation data from randomly spaced reporting stations is developed. The method is tested on 20 days of data from a network of 777 stations in the MAP3S study area in the northeastern {US}._
