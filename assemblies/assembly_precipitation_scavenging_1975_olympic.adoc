== Precipitation Scavenging Field Experiments in March 1975 on the Olympic Peninsula
[.big]#{CEH} and {JAY}

_Field operations during March 1975 which were a part of the precipitation scavenging research program are described, giving tentative conclusions regarding scavenging within weaker frontal storms and the use of chaff rockets in such experiments. Preliminary {doppd} velocity calculations from one storm are presented and the possible uses of this data discussed._
