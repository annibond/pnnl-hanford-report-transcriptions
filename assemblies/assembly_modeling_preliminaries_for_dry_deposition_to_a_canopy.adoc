= Modeling Preliminaries for Dry Deposition to a Canopy
W. G. N. Slinn

_Approximations are introduced to evaluate a canopy’s filtration efficiency. The results are illustrated by plotting the deposition velocity as a function of particle size for various values of the parameter γ = HB / (λp), B/p is the canopy packing density, H is the canopy height, and λ is a characteristic length of the collecting fibers._
