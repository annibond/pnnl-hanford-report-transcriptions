== Rain Scavenging of Active Aerosol Particles from a Plume
[.big]#{WGNS}#

_Hypotheses are advanced in an attempt to explain the measured rates at which particles are scavenged by rain from a kraft-process paper-mill plume. Weaknesses of the assumptions are noted as well as the value of further research._
