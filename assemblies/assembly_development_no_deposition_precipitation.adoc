== Development of a No-Deposition Precipitation Sampler
[.big]#{DWG}#

_A new type of field sampler is being developed at {BN} for precipitation chemistry studies under the {m3} program. This sampler incorporates sample freezing and automatic lid opening, and will be in operation for test purposes in the near future._
