== Rate Limiting Aspects of the Dry Deposition of Gases
[.big]#{WGNS}#

_Results from a simple model are presented which demonstrate that the flux of a pollutant gas from the atmosphere to a surface can be rate limited by a number of processes other than atmospheric diffusion._
