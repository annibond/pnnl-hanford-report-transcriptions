== A Design for Regional Precipitation Chemistry Measurement and Analysis
[.big]#{MTD} and {JMH}#

_A precipitation chemistry network, utilizing a newly designed collector, is being set up in the northeastern United States. Siting of three or four initial sites is being coordinated by NOAA, {ARL} personnel. Chemical analyses of samples will be done for all relevant fossil-fuel pollutant species, with careful quality control and rapid dissemination of results a major goal._
