== Resuspension Rates from a Circular Field Source
[.big]#{GAS} and {FDL}#

_Experiments are continuing in a lightly vegetated area to measure wind-caused particle resuspension rates of a controlled submicrometer inert tracer. Resuspension rates continue to reproducibly increase with wind speed for this aged source. The apparent availability for resuspension has not decreased for times up to 8 months._
