== An Experiment to Measure Particle Resuspension from a Large Annular Area
[.big]#{GAS} and {FDL}#

_Equipment installation was completed and the first experiment begin to measure inert particle resuspension rates from a 1031 m2 annular source. Mass budgets are being made with 108 air filter samplers mounted on six towers. Air is sampled as a function of wind direction and six wind speed increments._
