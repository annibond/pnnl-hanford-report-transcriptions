== Figure 1: Airborne Particle Volume Distribution (Neg 760188-5)

[#img-soil-insertion-particles]
[caption="Airborne Particle Volume Distribution", alt="{soil-insertion-particles-alt-1}"]
image::{soil-insertion-particles-img-1}[Airborne Particle Volume Distribution]

This graph shows the impact of the size of a particle within an isotype on the volume distribution _ΔV/Δ ln D_ in _μm^3^ / cm^3^_.

1. When the *wind speed* increases, the *particle volume* increases exponentially.

2. When the *sampling height* decreases, the *particle volume* increases exponentially.

3. The *upper and lower limits* represent the limits of the technology to detect data.

4. The peak of the *particle volume* is approximately ~ _20 through 50 μm_, representing the typical size of resuspended soil.
