.Concentration Versus Time During Tracer Release in Untable Atmospheres (Neg-72203-1)
image::../images/ref_variation_of_plume_centerline_concentration_with_time_figure-1.png[A pair of two graphs stacked on top of each other that show the concentration of tracer emissions in an unstable atmosphere. Both graphs show an electrical field strength of 2.7 x 10^-5^ over m^3^ and an initial tracer release of 200 meters high. A solid line (--) measures the history of exposure at a given distance, a dashed line (- - -) measures the exposure from a specific fence post for the electron positron, and the bar graph measures end-to-end peak concentrations for the plumes. The top graph (1) shows test V7. The x axis = time in seconds in increments of (1) 0.1 and (2) 0.10, although more time after 0.10 seconds is shown and unlabeled. The y axis = the relative concentration with increments of (1) 0.1, (2) 0.5, (3) 1.0, (4), 5.0, (5) 10.0, and (6) 20.0. The bottom graph (2) shows test V2. The x axis = time in minutes between 0 and 18 minutes in two minute increments. The y axis = the relative concentration with increments of (1) 0.2, (2) 0.5, (3) 1.0, (4) 5.0, (5) 10.0, (6) 20.0, and (7) 30.0.]

.Concentration Versus Time During Tracer Release in Stable Atmospheres (Neg-72203-2)
image::../images/ref_variation_of_plume_centerline_concentration_with_time_figure-2.png[A set of three graphs stacked on top of each other that show the concentration of tracer emissions in a stable atmosphere. All three graphs show an electrical field strength of 2.7 x 10^-5^ over m^3^. A solid line (--) measures the history of exposure at a given distance, a dashed line (- - -) measures the exposure from a specific fence post for the electron positron, and the bar graph measures end-to-end peak concentrations for the plumes. For all three graphs, the x axis = time in minutes between 0 and 24 minutes in increments of one minute. The y axis = the relative concentration in increments of (1) 0, (2) 0.5, (3) 1.0, (4) 2.0, and (5) 3.0. The top two graphs have an additional y-value of (7) 4.0. The top graph (1) shows test V6 with initial tracer release of 800 meters high. The middle graph (2) shows test V6 with an initial tracer release of 1600 meters high. The bottom graph (3) shows test V5 with an initial tracer release of 800 meters high.]
