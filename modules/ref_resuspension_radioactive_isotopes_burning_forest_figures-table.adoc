.Lapse Rates for Dry or Warm Air

[cols="1,1,1"]

|===

^|*Lapse Rate Name* ^|*Equation* ^|*Outcome*

|General Environmental Lapse Rate (ELR)
|stem:[\Gamma = -\frac{T_2 - T_1}{D_2 - D_1}]
|The actual, measurable temperature change of stationary air, used to determine the stability of the local atmosphere.

|Adiabatic Lapse Rate (DALR)
|stem:[\Gamma_d \approx 9.8 \text{°C/km}]
|The standard rate of cooling for rising dry air (approx. 5.4°F/1000ft). This is the "baseline" for dry climates like the Columbia Basin.

|Superadiabatic Lapse Rate 
|stem:[-\frac{\Delta T}{D} > \Gamma_d]
|A condition where the ground is so hot that air cools faster than the standard adiabatic rate. This creates the extreme buoyancy required for Dust Devils to form. 

|===
