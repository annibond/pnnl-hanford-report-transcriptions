.Greek Alphabet

[cols="1,1,1,1"]

|===

^|*α* (alpha)

alpha particle, two protons and two neutrons
^|*β* (beta)

beta particle, an electron emitted during beta decay
^|*γ* (gamma)

gamma readiation (type of radioactive decay)
^|*Δ* (Delta)

difference between two measurements

^|*δ* (delta)

an infitesimal difference or variation
^|*Ε* (Epsilon)

energy in Joules or an electrial field (uncommon)
^|*ε* (epsilon)

permittivity, electric field intensity, or emissivity
^|*Ζ* (Zeta)

atomic number of an element
^|*Φ* (theta)

the angle of particles scattered during a collison
^|*κ* (kappa)

coefficient of thermal conductivity 
^|*λ* (lambda)

decay constant (half-life)
^|*μ* (mu)

a prefix for micro (_10^-6^_)

^|*ν* (nu)

the mean of neutrons released per fission event
^|*π* (pi)

a subatomic particle that mediates nuclear parity
^|*ρ* (rho)

density
^|*Σ* (Sigma)

sum

^|*σ* (Tau)

the probability of a nuclear reaction occurring
^|*τ* (tau)

the mean lifetime of a radioactive isotope
^|*χ* (chi)

energy distribution of neutrons released during fission
^|*Ψ* (psi)

the wave function
^|*Ω* (Omega)

represents _Ω^-^, a subatomic particle containing three quarks
^|*ω*(omega)

angular frequency or a meson that mediates the nuclear force between nucleons

|===

NOTE: _β^+^_ represents a positron.

NOTE: _∂_ represents partial derivative, which is common in the formulas in this database. However, it is not a Greek letter.
