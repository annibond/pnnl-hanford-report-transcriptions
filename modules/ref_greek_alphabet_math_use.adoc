.Greek Letters & Definitions

[cols="1,1,1"]

|===

^|*Symbol* ^|*Greek Letter* ^|*Definition*

|α
|alpha (lowercase)
|alpha particle

|β
|beta (lowercase)
|beta particle

|γ
|gamma (lowercase)
|high-energy photons, a type of radioactive decay

|Δ, ∇
|Delta (Uppercase)
|finite difference

|δ
|delta (lowercase)
|a function that is zero everywhere except at the origin, where it is infinitely high

|ɛ̝
|epsilon (lowercase)
|a variable typically used to represent an arbitrarily small positive number or an error term

|θ
|theta (lowercase)
|the scattering angle of particles after they interact with a target nucleus

|Κ
|Kappa (uppercase)
|the state of a nuclear chain reaction

|κ
|kappa (lowercase)
|the neutron multiplication factor

|λ
|lambda (lowercase)
|decay constant, which represents the probability per unit time that a radioactive nucleus will decay

|μ
|mu (lowercase)
|the mean or the linear absorption coefficient in radiation physics

|ν
|nu (lowercase)
|the average number of neutrons released per fission event

|π
|pi (lowercase)
|pion, a type of subatomic particle that mediates the strong nuclear force binding protons and neutrons together

|Σ
|Sigma (uppercase)
|sum

|σ / ς
|sigma (lowercase)
|a measurement of the probability of a nuclear reaction occurring

|Τ
|Tau (uppercase)
|the mean lifetime of a radioactive substance

|τ
|tau (lowercase)
|the mean lifetime of a radioactive isotope

|Υ
|Upsilon (uppercase)
|a bound state of a bottom quark and a bottom antiquark

|Φ
|Phi (uppercase)
|a meson, a subatomic studied for its properties in nuclear matter (also known as a pion)

|φ / ϕ
|phi (lowercase)
|a meson, a subatomic studied for its properties in nuclear matter

|Χ
|Chi (uppercase)
|tensor

|χ
|chi (lowercase)
|square distribution

|Ω
|Omega (uppercase)
|angular frequency

|𝒪
|big O
|the upper bound of a function's growth rate

|σθū
|N/A
|covariance between the variable θ and a defined variable ū

|===
