== Figure 1: Schematic Showing Experimental Design for Particle Resuspension from a Cheat Grass Area Caused by Truck Passage (Neg 760188-15)

[#img-particle-truck-1]
[caption="Schematic Showing Experimental Design for Particle Resuspension", alt="{particle-truck-alt-1}"]
image::{particle-truck-img-1}[Schematic Showing Experimental Design for Particle Resuspension]

The setup of the diagram:

1. The truck described in the experiment parks at the beginning of a single-lane road.

2. The road is 10 feet wide and 50 feet long. It moves at a consistent speed. 

3. On the right side of the road, 7 air sampling towers take particle resuspension.

4. Wind blows from left to right in the direction of the tracers.

5. Samples are taken from the towers at heights of 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 8 feet high as the truck drives by.
