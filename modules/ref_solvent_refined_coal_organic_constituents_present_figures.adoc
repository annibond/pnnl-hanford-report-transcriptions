.Gas Chromatogram of Process Naphtha Arising in the Solvent Refining of Coal (Neg 759016-1)

image::../images/ref_solvent_refined_coal_organic_constituents_present_figure-1.png[A graph that displays the signal intensity of each of the chemical compounds separated by the SP-1000 at 10% from the process naphtha sample. The sample uses N~2~ as the carrier gas at a rate of 60 mL/min.  The x axis = the temperature in degrees Celsius in increments of (1) 100°, (2) 100°, (3) 150°, and (4) 200°C. The data continues after 200°C but evens out after that point. The first two increments on the x axis marked 100° represent the initial temperature of 100°, after which the temperature is held there as a constant for a specific period of time.  The y axis = the relative response. There are 22 data points which represent (1) 2-methylindan, (2) 5-methylindan, (3) 1, 3-dimethylindan, (4) 1, 2, 3 4-tetrahydronaphthalene, (5) 5-methyl-1, 2, 3, 4-tetrahydronaphthalene, (6) naphthalene, (7) 2-methylnaphthalene, (8) 1-methylnaphthalene, (9) 2, 6-dimethylnaphthalene, (10) phenol, o-cresol, (11) m-cresol, p-cresol, (12) 3, 5-xylenol, (13) acenaphthene, (14) dibenzofuran, (15) fluorene, (16) 9, 10-dihydrophenanthrene, (17) dibenzothiophene, (18) phenanthrene, anthracene, (19) 2-methylphenanthrene, (20) 1-methylphenanthrene, (21) fluoranthene, and (22) pyrene.]
