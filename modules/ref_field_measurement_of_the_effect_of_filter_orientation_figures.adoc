.The Ratio of Tracer Mass on Filters Facing Upward to Those Facing Toward Source as a Function of Wind Speed (Neg 72182-3)

image::../images/ref_field_measurement_of_the_effect_of_filter_orientation_figure-1.png[A dotted graph that shows the measurement of tracer concentration when facing AWAY from the mean wind direction and the concentration by the filter facing TOWARD the mean wind direction with a wind speed of 2 meters high. There is an inverse correlation between wind speed and the concentration ratio. The x axis = wind speed in meters at 2 meters of elevation between 0 and 7 in meters per second in one meter increments. The y axis = the concentration ratio of the AWAY and TOWARD forces of the tracer nucleon between 0 and 1.2 in increments of 0.1.]
