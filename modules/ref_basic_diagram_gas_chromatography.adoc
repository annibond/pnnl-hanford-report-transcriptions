A block diagram of a *gas chromatography system*, showing the flow path:

1. from the *carrier gas tank (a)* through the *pressure and flow controllers (b & c)*

2. from the pressure and flow controllers* to the *sample injection port (d)*

3. from the sample injection port to the *column oven (i)* containing *analytical columns (e)*

4. from the column oven's analytical colums to the *detector (f)* and the *process signal (g)* 

5. connected to the *outlet (h)*
