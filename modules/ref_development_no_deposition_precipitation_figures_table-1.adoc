.Sites for Wet and Dry Scavenging in the Eastern {US}

[cols="1,1"]

|===

^|*Term* ^|*Definition*

|*Wet*
|Precipitation scavenging, particulate that are washed out of the air by a form of water (e.g., rain or snow).

|*Dry*
|Deposition from dry material (e.g., dust) that fall or stick to the surfaces when there is no precipitation.

|*Bulk*
|Samplers that stay open to catch both types of material.

|===
