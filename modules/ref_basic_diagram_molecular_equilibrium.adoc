*Key*

* *positrons* are purple

* *neutrons* are blue.

A diagram showing the transfer of individual particles between two larger clusters. The isotope on the left latexmath:[14_8^Z] where _Z_ represents the element or isotope. This process stabilizes the isotope.

1. during molecular equilibrium _Z-14_ *loses one positron*

2. the *lost positron* becomes a *neutron*

3. _Z-14_ becomes latexmath:[14_7^Z]
