== Important Names & Locations to Hanford

[cols="1,1,1"]

|===

^|*Name* ^|*Location* ^|*Purpose*

|Atomic Energy Commission (AEC)
|Germantown, MD
|A former {US} federal agency established after {WW2} in 1946 to manage the development of atomic sciences and techology, later merged with the {US} Energy Research and Development Administration in 1975

|{bat} Memorial Institute
|Columbus, OH (primary)
|nonprofit research and development organization that contributed to the Manhattan Project

|Energy Research and Development Administration (ERDA)
|{WA}, D.C.
|A former {US} federal agency established from the split of the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) 1975, later merged with the {US} Department of Energy in 1977

|Montana's Malmstrom Air Force Base
|Colstrip, Mo
|Location of multiple intercontinetal ballistic missle silos (ICBMs) and site of significant nuclear testing radioactive fallout

|Green Glow Program
|{rich}, WA
|The Green Glow Diffusion Program at the Hanford site was a series of atmospheric field diffusion experiments conducted in 1959 to test techniques for tracking airborne materials.

|{HNR}
|{rich}, WA
|site for nuclear testing research and plutonium production during {WW2} and the Cold War, part of the Manhattan Project

|Liquid-Metal Fast-Breeder Reactor (LMFBR)
|{US}, as well as Canada, China, France, India, Japan, Russia, and {UK}
|the Experimental Breeder Reactor II (EBR-II) and Fermi 1 in the {US}, a three loop system in which liquid sodium cool with an intermediate loop containing non-radioactive sodium transfer heat to a steam generator

|Manhattan Project
|{US}, {UK}, and Canada
|research and development program undertaken during {WW2} to produce nuclear weapons on the side of the Allies, directed by {US} Major General Leslie Groves

|{NAAQS} (NAAQS)
|{WA} D.C.
|regulations set by the {US} Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to protect public health from carbon monoxide, lead, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, particulate matter, and sulfure dioxide

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Plr7_SAuB6g[EPA, EPA, EPA!]

|{NARL}
|{PB}, AK
|site for nuclear testing on nearby Amchitka Island, which hosted Project Long Shot (1965) and Project Milrow (1969)

|{PNL}
|{rich}, WA
|today known as the {PNNL} (PNNL), managed by the {DOE}, distinct from {HNR} as an employer

|{quill} Air Reference Station
|{quill}, WA
|former military air station and current air sampling and monitoring station, named for the federally recognized Native American people, pronounced KWIL-yoot 

|Solvent Refined Coal (SRC) Pilot Plant
|Fort Lewis, WA
|A pilot plant that operated from 1974-1981 by the {PMCMC}, a temporary military installation to test the process of converting coal into a cleaner burning fuel

|{DOE}
|{WA} D.C.
|department of the federal government that oversees national energy policy, the development of nuclear power, and the military's weapons program for the {US}

|{US} Radium Corporation
|Orange, NJ
|a coporation founded in 1914 that produced uranium and developed radioluminescent paint, responsible for the infamous Radium Girls case in which workers in their plant died slowly from radiation poisoning
