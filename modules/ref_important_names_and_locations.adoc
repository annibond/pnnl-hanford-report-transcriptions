== Important Names & Locations to Hanford

[cols="1,1,1"]

|===

^|*Name* ^|*Location* ^|*Purpose*

|Atomic Energy Commission (AEC)
|Germantown, MD
|Former {US} federal agency established after {WW2} in 1946 to manage the development of atomic sciences and techology, later merged with the {US} Energy Research and Development Administration in 1975

|{bat} Memorial Institute
|Columbus, OH (primary)
|Nonprofit research and development organization that contributed to the Manhattan Project

|{DNA}
|{DC}
|Former {US} federal agency established after the {CW} under the {DOE} that handled the testing, safety, and management of nuclear weapons, which later became the {US} {DTRA}

|Energy Research and Development Administration (ERDA)
|{WA}, D.C.
|Former {US} federal agency established from the split of the Atomic Energy Commission (AEC) 1975, later merged with the {US} Department of Energy in 1977

|Gifford Pinchot National Forest
|near Amboy, {WA}, in the {sw} part of the state
|National forest in southern {WA} managed by the {USFS} that extends along the {CMR}, including {mt-helens}, {mt-ranier}, and the {col-river}.

|Green Glow Program
|{rich}, WA
|The Green Glow Diffusion Program at the Hanford site was a series of atmospheric field diffusion experiments conducted in 1959 to test techniques for tracking airborne materials, now known as the Green Run experiment

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Run[Wikipedia - Green Run]

|{HNR}
|{rich}, WA
|Site for nuclear testing research and plutonium production during {WW2} and the Cold War, part of the Manhattan Project

|Liquid-Metal Fast-Breeder Reactor (LMFBR)
|{US}, as well as Canada, China, France, India, Japan, Russia, and {UK}
|The Experimental Breeder Reactor II (EBR-II) and Fermi 1 in the {US}, a three loop system in which liquid sodium cool with an intermediate loop containing non-radioactive sodium transfer heat to a steam generator

|Manhattan Project
|{US}, {UK}, and Canada
|Research and development program undertaken during {WW2} to produce nuclear weapons on the side of the Allies, directed by {US} Major General Leslie Groves

|Montana's Malmstrom Air Force Base
|Colstrip, Mo
|Location of multiple intercontinetal ballistic missle silos (ICBMs) and site of significant nuclear testing radioactive fallout

|{mt-helens} (Mt. St. Helens)
|Skamania County, {WA}
|Active volcano a part of the {CMR}, 98 miles (_~ 157.72 km_) south of {sea} and 52 (_~ 83.69 km_) {ne} of {port}, {OR}, named Lawetlat'la to the local Cowlitz nation (pronounced lah-weight-lot-lah)

|{NAAQS} (NAAQS)
|{DC}
|Set of regulations set by the {US} Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) to protect public health from carbon monoxide, lead, nitrogen dioxide, ozone, particulate matter, and sulfure dioxide

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Plr7_SAuB6g[EPA, EPA, EPA!]

|{NARL}
|{PB}, AK
|Site for nuclear testing on nearby Amchitka Island, which hosted Project Long Shot (1965) and Project Milrow (1969)

|{NOAA} (NOAA)
|{boul}, {CO}
|{US} federal agency that monitors, predicts, and manages the Earth's atmosphere, oceans, and coasts, with multiple agencies underneath it set to shut down between 2028 and 2029 under the second term of the Trump Administration

|{NTIS}
|{alex}, {VA}
|Agency within the {DOC} that serves as a central repository for scientific, technical, and business information uncovered by research govered by the {US} government with the aim of making that knowledge accessible to corporations, academic institutions, and potential entrepreneurs and "innovators"

|{NWS} (NWS)
|Silver Spring, {MD}
|Federal agency under the {NOAA} that provides weather, water, and climate forecasts, warning, and data for the {US} and its territories, with many of its contracts terminated under the second term of the Trump Administration in March and April 2025

|{oz-lake}
|Makah Indian Reservation in Clallam County
|Lake that served as a zero point for incoming air from the {OP}. Its "clean" air makes it as close to a neutral starting point as the most neutral place researchers could use through the {PNL}'s sensors

|{PNL}
|{rich}, WA
|Research organization today known as the {PNNL} (PNNL), managed by the {DOE}, distinct from {HNR} as an employer

|{quil} Air Reference Station
|{quil}, WA
|Former military air station and current air sampling and monitoring station, named for the federally recognized Native American people, pronounced KWIL-yoot

|Retec Development Laboratory (Retec)
|Portland, OR
|Manufacturer that produced devices including nebulizers and therapeutic breathing devices

|Solvent Refined Coal (SRC) Pilot Plant
|Fort Lewis, WA
|A pilot plant that operated from 1974-1981 by the {PMCMC}, a temporary military installation to test the process of converting coal into a cleaner burning fuel

|{DOE}
|{WA} D.C.
|Department of the federal government that oversees national energy policy, the development of nuclear power, and the military's weapons program for the {US}

|{USFS} (USFS)
|{DC}, {port}, {OR}
|Agency founded in 1905 that operates under the {US} {DOA} and manages the nation's 154 national forests and 20 national grasslands. The R6 range exclusively covers {OR} and {WA}.

|{US} Radium Corporation
|Orange, NJ
|a coporation founded in 1914 that produced uranium and developed radioluminescent paint, responsible for the infamous Radium Girls case in which workers in their plant died slowly from radiation poisoning

https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radium_Girls[Radium Girls]

|{WPL} (WPL)
|{boul}, {CO} (historical)
{hous}, {TX}
|Federal agency under the {NOAA} that investigates how energy waves (i.e., acoustic, seismic, optical, or mechanical) travel through different mediums, unknown status of its current government contract or expiration datea

|===

NOTE: Conversions between miles and kilometers are rounded to the second decimal point due to many of the papers in this series being limited to the same decimal point. Sometimes Imperial measurements are used in these papers, as Hanford published the first Cold War research in a desert climate.
