Radioactive decay is a spontaneous process in which a nuclide or isotope loses energy by emitting radiation to become more stable.

* Isotopes want to stabilize.

* Radioactive decay is spontaneous and random.

* The length of the decay is based on the element's half life.

* There are three types of radioactive decay:

- *Alpha (α) decay:* Releases a helium nucleus (2 protons and 2 neutrons)

- *Beta (β) decay:* Releases a beta particle (electron or positron), which transforms a neutron or a proton

- *Gamma (γ) decay:* Releases photons to shed excess energy
