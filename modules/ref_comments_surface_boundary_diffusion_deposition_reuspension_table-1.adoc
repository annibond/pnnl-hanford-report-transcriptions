^|*Options* ^|*Interrelations and Comments*

2+<.^|*1. Specification of the jump discontinuity*

| a. Gases (liquid absorber)
| stem:[\chi_o = Hc_o], where stem:[H] = Henry's law constant

| b. Particles
| stem:[\chi_o = Jc_o] +
  stem:[\chi_o = KG(a)], where stem:[K \cong J / \delta] is the resuspension factor (b)

2+<.^| *2. Specification of the magnitude of the common Flux, F:*

| a. Gases (liquid absorber)
| stem:[F = \lambda(c_o - c_{\lambda b})] +
  stem:[F = k_a (\chi_{ab} - \chi_o)] +
  stem:[F = k_o (\chi_{ab} - Hc_{\lambda b})], where stem:[K_{\lambda} \cong D_z / \delta], stem:[k_a \cong K_z / H], and stem:[k_o^{-1} = k_a^{-1} + \lambda^{-1}]

| b. Particles
| stem:[F = v_d \chi_o] +
  stem:[F = v_d \chi_o - v_r c_o] +
  stem:[F = v_d \chi_o - \Lambda G] +
  stem:[F = \Omega A - \lambda G(a)], where stem:[\infty < v_d > ?]

| *Definitions (b)*
| stem:[v_r] = resuspension velocity +
  stem:[\Delta \cong v_r / \delta] = resuspension rate +
  stem:[\Omega \cong v_d / H] = deposition rate
